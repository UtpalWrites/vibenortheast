1. Magical Meghalaya Explorer

Guwahati • Shillong • Cherrapunjee • Dawki • Mawlynnong

4 Nights / 5 Days

Day-by-Day Itinerary

Places Included: Umiam Lake, Police Bazar, Elephant Falls, Shillong Peak, Laitlum Canyon, Nohkalikai Falls, Seven Sisters Falls, Mawsmai & Arwah Caves, Mawlynnong (Cleanest Village), Single Root Bridge, Umngot River (Dawki), Krang Suri Falls.
Day 1: Guwahati to Shillong

Pickup from Guwahati Airport/Railway Station. Drive to Shillong with a scenic stop at Umiam Lake (Barapani). Evening visit to Police Bazar. Night stay in Shillong.

Day 2: Shillong to Cherrapunjee

Visit Elephant Falls, Shillong Peak, and breathtaking Laitlum Canyon. Proceed to Cherrapunjee covering Mawkdok Valley, Seven Sisters Falls, Nohkalikai Falls, Mawsmai Cave & Arwah Cave. Night stay in Cherrapunjee.

Day 3: Mawlynnong & Dawki Tour

Drive to Mawlynnong (Asia's Cleanest Village) and walk across the Single Decker Living Root Bridge. Later head to Dawki (Umngot River) for crystal-clear boating & see the Indo-Bangladesh border. Return to Shillong for night stay.

Day 4: Jowai & Krang Suri Excursion

Full-day excursion to the magical blue waters of Krang Suri Waterfalls, Phe Phe Falls, and Nartiang Monoliths. Evening return to Shillong.

Day 5: Shillong Sightseeing & Guwahati Drop

Morning visit to Ward's Lake and Don Bosco Museum. Drive back to Guwahati Airport/Station for onward journey.

2. Arunachal & Kaziranga Expedition

Guwahati • Kaziranga • Dirang • Tawang • Bumla Pass • Bomdila

6 Nights / 7 Days

Day-by-Day Itinerary

Places Included: Kaziranga National Park, Orchid Park, Tipi Orchidarium, Dirang Monastery, Sangti Valley, Sela Pass, Sela Lake, Jaswant Garh War Memorial, Jung Falls, Tawang Monastery, Giant Buddha Statue, Tawang War Memorial, Bumla Pass, Madhuri Lake, Bomdila Monastery.
Day 1: Guwahati to Kaziranga

Pickup from Guwahati and drive to Kaziranga National Park. Afternoon visit to the Kaziranga Orchid & Biodiversity Park and local tea estates. Night stay in Kaziranga.

Day 2: Kaziranga Safari to Dirang

Early morning thrilling Elephant / Jeep Safari to spot the One-Horned Rhino. After breakfast, drive to Dirang crossing Bhalukpong border and Tipi Orchidarium. Night stay in Dirang.

Day 3: Dirang to Tawang via Sela Pass

Visit Sangti Valley and Dirang Monastery. Drive to Tawang crossing the snow-clad Sela Pass (13,700 ft), Sela Lake, Jaswant Garh War Memorial, and Nuranang (Jung) Falls. Night stay in Tawang.

Day 4: Tawang & Bumla Pass Excursion

Explore Bumla Pass (Indo-China Border) and Sangestar Tso (Madhuri Lake). Afternoon visit to Tawang Monastery, Giant Buddha Statue, and evening Light & Sound show at Tawang War Memorial.

Day 5: Tawang to Bomdila

Drive back towards Bomdila enjoying panoramic Himalayan mountain views. Visit Bomdila Monastery and local craft center. Night stay in Bomdila.

Day 6: Bomdila to Guwahati

Scenic descent from the mountains back to the plains of Assam via Kalaktang/Shergaon route. Evening arrival in Guwahati for night stay.

Day 7: Kamakhya Darshan & Departure

Morning visit to Maa Kamakhya Temple for Darshan, followed by drop at Guwahati Airport/Railway Station.

3. Glorious Assam: Spiritual, Wildlife & Heritage

Guwahati • Kamakhya • Kaziranga • Majuli Island • Sivasagar

5 Nights / 6 Days

Day-by-Day Itinerary

Places Included: Maa Kamakhya Temple, Umananda Island, Balaji Temple, Brahmaputra Sunset Cruise, Maha Mrityunjay Temple, Kaziranga National Park, Majuli River Island (Vaishnavite Satras & Mask Making), Sivasagar (Rang Ghar, Talatal Ghar, Sivadol), Jorhat Tea Gardens.
Day 1: Guwahati Arrival & Spiritual Tour

Pickup from Airport/Station. Visit Maa Kamakhya Temple, ferry to Umananda Island, and Tirupati Balaji Temple. Evening Brahmaputra River Cruise. Night stay in Guwahati.

Day 2: Guwahati to Kaziranga via Nagaon

Drive to Kaziranga. En route visit the world's largest Shivling at Maha Mrityunjay Temple in Nagaon. Evening cultural show at Orchid Park. Night stay in Kaziranga.

Day 3: Kaziranga Safari to Majuli Island

Morning Jeep/Elephant Safari in Kaziranga. Afternoon drive to Nimati Ghat (Jorhat) and board the ferry to Majuli (World's Largest River Island). Night stay in Majuli.

Day 4: Majuli Satras to Sivasagar / Jorhat

Explore Samaguri Satra (Mask Making), Auniati Satra, and Kamalabari Satra. Take afternoon ferry back and drive to Sivasagar/Jorhat for night stay.

Day 5: Sivasagar Ahom Heritage to Guwahati

Explore royal Ahom monuments: Rang Ghar (Asia's oldest amphitheater), Talatal Ghar, and Sivadol. Drive back to Guwahati for night stay.

Day 6: Local Shopping & Departure

Morning visit to Assam Silk (Muga/Pat) & Tea markets, followed by drop at Guwahati Airport/Railway Station.

Explore The Destinations

Kamakhya Temple

One of the most powerful Shakti Peethas situated atop Nilachal Hill. Famous for the Ambubachi Mela.

Umananda Island

The smallest inhabited river island in the world, located in the middle of the Brahmaputra River.

Panimur Waterfalls

Known as the 'Niagara of Assam,' this breathtaking waterfall on the Kopili River offers a mesmerizing view.

Charaideo Maidam

The sacred burial grounds of the Ahom Kings, often compared to the Pyramids of Egypt. A UNESCO site.

Majuli

The world's largest river island and the cultural capital of Assam. Famous for its Vaishnavite Satras.

Manas National Park

A UNESCO World Heritage site known for its Project Tiger reserve and wild water buffaloes.

Tea Gardens

Walk through the lush green carpet of Assam's famous tea estates and stay in heritage bungalows.

Rang Ghar

Asia's oldest amphitheater, used by Ahom royals. A magnificent two-story red brick structure in Sivasagar.

Seven Sisters Falls

A spectacular seven-segmented waterfall located near Cherrapunjee.

Mawsmai Cave

A thrilling limestone cave experience with narrow passages and unique stalactite formations.

Laitlum Canyon

Meaning 'End of Hills,' this canyon offers breathtaking panoramic views of deep gorges.

Shillong

Known as the 'Scotland of the East' for its rolling hills and colonial charm.

Dawki River

Famous for the Umngot River with its crystal clear waters where boats appear to float on air.

Mawlynnong

Acclaimed as Asia's cleanest village. Visitors can enjoy the Sky Walk and local culture.

Living Root Bridge

A marvel of bio-engineering created by Khasi tribes using rubber tree roots.

Nohkalikai Falls

The tallest plunge waterfall in India at 340 meters, featuring a stunning blue pool.

One Horned Rhino

Kaziranga hosts two-thirds of the world's Great One-Horned Rhinoceros population.

Spotted Deer

The park is home to large herds of Eastern Swamp Deer and Hog Deer.

Wild Water Buffalo

Kaziranga has the largest population of Wild Water Buffalo globally.

Kaziranga Orchids

Visit the Orchid and Biodiversity Park to see over 600 varieties of wild orchids.

Jeep Safari

Experience the thrill of the jungle with an open jeep safari to spot wildlife up close.

Madhuri Lake

This high-altitude lake was formed by an earthquake and offers stunning alpine views.

Mechuka Valley

Often called the 'Mini Switzerland' of Arunachal with snow-capped peaks and pine forests.

Bumla Pass

A historic mountain pass at the Indo-China border at 15,200 ft.

Tawang Monastery

India's largest and the world's second-largest monastery, perched at 10,000 ft.

Sela Pass

A high-altitude mountain pass that serves as the stunning gateway to Tawang.

Ziro Valley

A lush green valley home to the Apatani tribe, famous for their unique culture.

Dzukou Valley

Known as the 'Valley of Flowers of the Northeast.' A trekker's paradise.

Japfu Peak

The second highest peak in Nagaland, offering stunning views of Dzukou Valley.

Khonoma Village

Asia's first Green Village, famous for its history and modern conservation efforts.

Hornbill Festival

The 'Festival of Festivals' showcasing the rich culture of all Naga tribes.

Kohima

The historic capital city, famous for the WWII War Cemetery.

Shree Govindajee

A historic Vaishnavite temple located next to the royal palace in Imphal.

Sadu Chiru Falls

A picturesque spot near Imphal requiring a short hike through lush greenery.

Keibul Lamjao

The world's only floating National Park and home of the endangered Sangai.

Loktak Lake

The largest freshwater lake in Northeast India, famous for floating swamps.

Kangla Fort

The ancient seat of the Meitei rulers in the heart of Imphal.

Aizawl

The scenic capital city perched on vertical ridges overlooking the valley.

Reiek Tlang

A mystic mountain peak offering breathtaking panoramic views.

Dampa Tiger Reserve

A dense jungle sanctuary perfect for nature enthusiasts.

Phawngpui Peak

Known as the Blue Mountain, famous for its orchids and stunning cliff views.

Vantawng Falls

The highest and most spectacular waterfall in Mizoram.
