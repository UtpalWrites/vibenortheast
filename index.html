Magical Meghalaya Explorer

Guwahati • Shillong • Cherrapunjee • Dawki • Mawlynnong • Krang Suri

4 Nights / 5 Days

Day-by-Day Itinerary

Places Included: Umiam Lake, Police Bazar, Elephant Falls, Shillong Peak, Laitlum Canyon, Nohkalikai Falls, Seven Sisters Falls, Mawsmai & Arwah Caves, Mawlynnong, Single Root Bridge, Dawki River, Krang Suri Falls.
Day 1: Guwahati to Shillong

Pickup from Guwahati Airport/Station. Stop at Umiam Lake. Evening stroll at Police Bazar. Night stay in Shillong.

Day 2: Shillong to Cherrapunjee

Visit Elephant Falls, Shillong Peak & Laitlum Canyon. Proceed to Cherrapunjee covering Mawkdok Valley, Seven Sisters Falls, Nohkalikai Falls, Mawsmai & Arwah Caves.

Day 3: Mawlynnong & Dawki Tour

Visit Mawlynnong (Asia's Cleanest Village) & Living Root Bridge. Head to Dawki (Umngot River) for boating & Indo-Bangladesh border view. Return to Shillong.

Day 4: Jowai & Krang Suri Excursion

Full-day trip to the crystal-blue Krang Suri Waterfalls, Phe Phe Falls & Nartiang Monoliths. Evening return to Shillong.

Day 5: Shillong Sightseeing & Guwahati Drop

Morning visit to Ward's Lake & Don Bosco Museum. Drive back to Guwahati Airport/Station for departure.

Arunachal & Kaziranga Expedition

Guwahati • Kaziranga • Dirang • Tawang • Bumla Pass • Bomdila

6 Nights / 7 Days

Day-by-Day Itinerary

Places Included: Kaziranga National Park, Orchid Park, Tipi Orchidarium, Dirang Monastery, Sangti Valley, Sela Pass, Sela Lake, Jaswant Garh, Jung Falls, Tawang Monastery, Giant Buddha Statue, Bumla Pass, Madhuri Lake, Bomdila Monastery.
Day 1: Guwahati to Kaziranga

Pickup from Guwahati & drive to Kaziranga. Visit Orchid & Biodiversity Park and tea gardens. Night stay in Kaziranga.

Day 2: Kaziranga Safari to Dirang

Early morning Elephant / Jeep Safari. Drive to Dirang via Bhalukpong & Tipi Orchidarium. Night stay in Dirang.

Day 3: Dirang to Tawang via Sela Pass

Visit Sangti Valley & Dirang Monastery. Cross Sela Pass (13,700 ft), Sela Lake, Jaswant Garh & Jung Falls. Night stay in Tawang.

Day 4: Tawang & Bumla Pass Excursion

Visit Bumla Pass & Madhuri Lake. Afternoon tour of Tawang Monastery, Giant Buddha Statue & War Memorial.

Day 5: Tawang to Bomdila

Scenic drive to Bomdila. Visit Bomdila Monastery and local handicraft market. Night stay in Bomdila.

Day 6: Bomdila to Guwahati

Descent from the Eastern Himalayas back to Guwahati via Kalaktang/Shergaon route. Night stay in Guwahati.

Day 7: Kamakhya Darshan & Departure

Morning Darshan at Maa Kamakhya Temple, followed by Airport/Station drop.

Glorious Assam: Spiritual, Wildlife & Heritage

Guwahati • Kamakhya • Kaziranga • Majuli Island • Sivasagar

5 Nights / 6 Days

Day-by-Day Itinerary

Places Included: Maa Kamakhya Temple, Umananda Island, Balaji Temple, Brahmaputra Cruise, Maha Mrityunjay Temple, Kaziranga National Park, Majuli Satras, Rang Ghar, Talatal Ghar, Sivadol.
Day 1: Guwahati Arrival & Spiritual Tour

Visit Maa Kamakhya Temple, Umananda Island & Balaji Temple. Evening Brahmaputra Sunset Cruise.

Day 2: Guwahati to Kaziranga via Nagaon

Drive to Kaziranga with a stop at Maha Mrityunjay Temple. Evening cultural show at Orchid Park.

Day 3: Kaziranga Safari to Majuli Island

Morning Jeep/Elephant Safari. Drive to Nimati Ghat & take the ferry to Majuli River Island.

Day 4: Majuli Satras to Sivasagar

Explore Samaguri Satra (Mask Making), Auniati & Kamalabari Satra. Ferry back & drive to Sivasagar/Jorhat.

Day 5: Sivasagar Ahom Heritage to Guwahati

Visit Rang Ghar, Talatal Ghar & Sivadol. Drive back to Guwahati for night stay.

Day 6: Silk Shopping & Departure

Morning visit to Assam Silk & Tea markets, followed by drop at Airport/Railway Station.

Explore The Destinations

Kamakhya Temple

One of the most powerful Shakti Peethas situated atop Nilachal Hill.

Umananda Island

The smallest inhabited river island in the world, in the Brahmaputra River.

Panimur Waterfalls

Known as the 'Niagara of Assam' on the Kopili River.

Charaideo Maidam

Sacred burial grounds of the Ahom Kings. A UNESCO World Heritage site.

Majuli

The world's largest river island and the cultural capital of Assam.

Manas National Park

A UNESCO World Heritage Tiger Reserve and wildlife sanctuary.

Tea Gardens

Walk through the lush green carpet of Assam's iconic tea estates.

Rang Ghar

Asia's oldest amphitheater built by the Ahom royals in Sivasagar.

Seven Sisters Falls

A spectacular seven-segmented waterfall near Cherrapunjee.

Mawsmai Cave

A thrilling limestone cave with unique stalactite formations.

Laitlum Canyon

'End of Hills' offering breathtaking panoramic views of deep gorges.

Shillong

Known as the 'Scotland of the East' for its rolling hills and charm.

Dawki River

Crystal clear Umngot River waters where boats appear to float on air.

Mawlynnong

Acclaimed as Asia's cleanest village with its famous Sky Walk.

Living Root Bridge

A bio-engineering marvel woven by Khasi tribes from rubber tree roots.

Nohkalikai Falls

India's tallest plunge waterfall at 340 meters with a turquoise pool.

One Horned Rhino

Home to two-thirds of the world's Great One-Horned Rhinoceros population.

Spotted Deer

Large herds of Eastern Swamp Deer and Hog Deer across the grasslands.

Wild Water Buffalo

Kaziranga holds the largest population of Wild Water Buffalo globally.

Kaziranga Orchids

Over 600 varieties of wild orchids at the Biodiversity Park.

Jeep Safari

Thrilling open-jeep safaris to spot wildlife up close.

Madhuri Lake

High-altitude alpine lake surrounded by snow-dusted peaks.

Mechuka Valley

The 'Mini Switzerland' of Arunachal with pine forests and rivers.

Bumla Pass

Historic mountain pass at the Indo-China border at 15,200 ft.

Tawang Monastery

India's largest monastery, perched at 10,000 ft.

Sela Pass

Stunning high-altitude gateway to Tawang.

Ziro Valley

Lush pine valley home to the unique Apatani tribal culture.

Dzukou Valley

The 'Valley of Flowers of the Northeast'—a trekker's paradise.

Japfu Peak

Nagaland's second highest peak overlooking Dzukou Valley.

Khonoma Village

Asia's first Green Village known for conservation and heritage.

Hornbill Festival

The 'Festival of Festivals' celebrating all Naga tribes.

Kohima

Historic capital city famous for the WWII War Cemetery.

Shree Govindajee

Historic Vaishnavite temple beside the royal palace in Imphal.

Sadu Chiru Falls

Picturesque waterfall nestled in lush green hills near Imphal.

Keibul Lamjao

The world's only floating National Park and home of the Sangai deer.

Loktak Lake

Northeast India's largest freshwater lake famous for its phumdis.

Kangla Fort

The ancient seat of the Meitei rulers in Imphal.

Aizawl

Scenic hill capital perched on steep vertical ridges.

Reiek Tlang

Mystic mountain peak offering sweeping valley panoramas.

Dampa Tiger Reserve

Dense tropical sanctuary ideal for nature lovers.

Phawngpui Peak

The Blue Mountain, celebrated for its orchids and cliff views.

Vantawng Falls

Mizoram's highest and most spectacular waterfall.
